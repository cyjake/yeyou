# Private beta test kit

Milestone 6 is a field activity, not a coding checkbox. The product is ready to run it; completion requires observing real participants.

## Participants

Recruit two overlapping groups:

- 5–8 Japanese learners who care about reading accuracy.
- 5–8 visually motivated creators who already post literary, anime-adjacent or reflective text content.

Do not require accounts. Test on participants’ normal phones when possible.

## 15-minute session

1. Ask the participant to choose a built-in line or paste a line they already like.
2. Do not explain the controls unless they are blocked for more than 30 seconds.
3. Ask them to make something they would plausibly save or share.
4. If Japanese is used, ask them to inspect and, if needed, correct the reading.
5. Ask them to export the result.
6. Invite a second composition only after the first export; do not prompt them toward it.

## Observe

- Seconds to first meaningful preview.
- Seconds to first export.
- Whether they started from the gallery or pasted text.
- Where they hesitated.
- Whether a reading was corrected and whether the correction UI was understood.
- Whether they exported, shared, or voluntarily made a second card.
- The exported artifact itself, with permission.

The app keeps only device-local aggregate counters for gallery opens, example use and successful export. These support the conversation but do not replace observation.

## Five closing questions

1. What did you expect to happen that did not happen?
2. Which part felt most like “yours”?
3. Was anything about the text or reading untrustworthy?
4. Where would you share this, if anywhere?
5. Would you make another one this week? Why or why not?

## Decision gate

Proceed with deeper content, templates and AI assistance when participants voluntarily export/share or make a second card. If they mainly experiment with controls, revisit the content-to-template loop before adding more controls.

Record fixes separately from observations. The first pass after this milestone is the refinement session: correctness bugs first, then blocked flows, then visual polish.
